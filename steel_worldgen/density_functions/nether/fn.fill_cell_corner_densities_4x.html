SIMD form of fill_cell_corner_densities that batches 4 cell-corner Y values at fixed (x, z).

out layout: lane-major SoA. Lane i’s INTERPOLATED_COUNT channels live at out[i * INTERPOLATED_COUNT..(i + 1) * INTERPOLATED_COUNT]. out must have length 4 * INTERPOLATED_COUNT.

Per-lane semantics are bit-identical to four scalar fill_cell_corner_densities calls at the same Y values.
